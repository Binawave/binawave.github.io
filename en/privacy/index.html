Privacy Policy

Binawave handles information needed for the app while keeping data on your device where possible. This policy explains app data, HealthKit, and Premium purchase information.

Last updated: 2026-09-07

1. Information Stored

  • Volume, base tone, brainwave mode, and timer settings
  • User presets
  • Session logs
  • Settings related to Premium availability

If you enable Health integration, Binawave uses HealthKit to handle the start and end time needed to save mindful sessions.

2. Purpose of Use

  • Keeping your app settings, presets, and session history
  • Checking and restoring Premium purchase status
  • Health integration only when you explicitly allow it

3. Storage and Retention

Settings, presets, and session logs are stored on your device to provide app functionality. This version does not include code that automatically sends them to a Binawave-operated server. Device data may be removed when you delete the app or erase device data.

HealthKit records are managed in Apple's Health environment. Their retention and deletion follow your Health settings.

4. HealthKit Integration

Writing to the Health app happens only when you allow it through the iOS permission dialog. Binawave saves only the start and end time needed for mindful session records.

HealthKit permissions can be changed at any time in iOS Settings or the Health app.

5. External Transmission and Third Parties

This version of the app does not include code that automatically sends your settings or session logs to an independent server.

Purchase processing and billing information are managed by Apple. Binawave does not store credit card information.

The app and this website do not include Binawave-operated advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics tags, or tracking mechanisms. If you contact support, we use the message and email address you provide to respond.

Except where required by law, Binawave does not sell or provide personal data to third parties without user action.

6. User Control

  • Health records can be reviewed and deleted in the Health app
  • Settings and session logs stored on your device may be lost if you delete the app or erase device data

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when features, laws, or service operations change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised last-updated date.

8. Contact

Support email: support@binawave.app

Support URL: https://binawave.app/en/support/
